# User Guide: FB AdBlocker & Clean Feed

Welcome! This guide explains how to install, use, and customize the extension in simple, plain language.

---

## 📥 Installation Step-by-Step

### 1. Download
1. Head to the GitHub repository page.
2. Click the green **Code** button and select **Download ZIP**.
3. Extract (unzip) the file on your computer.

### 2. Install in Your Browser

#### For Google Chrome:
1. Open Chrome and go to `chrome://extensions`.
2. Turn on **Developer mode** in the top right corner.
3. Click the **Load unpacked** button on the top left.
4. Choose the folder you extracted (the one containing `manifest.json`).

#### For Microsoft Edge:
1. Open Edge and go to `edge://extensions`.
2. Toggle on **Developer mode** on the left menu.
3. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the extracted folder.

#### For Brave Browser:
1. Open Brave and go to `brave://extensions`.
2. Turn on **Developer mode** in the top right.
3. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the folder.

---

## ⚙️ How to Customize Your Settings

### Opening the Menu
1. Click the puzzle icon (🧩) in the top right of your browser toolbar.
2. Click the pin icon next to **FB AdBlocker & Clean Feed** to keep it visible.
3. Click the extension icon to open the quick toggle menu.

### Blocking Words You Don't Want to See
You can filter out topics that annoy you (such as spoilers, politics, or celebrities):
1. Open the extension popup.
2. Click **Keywords & Advanced Settings** at the bottom.
3. Under **Custom Keyword Blocker**, type a word or phrase (e.g., `spoilers`, `crypto`, `politics`).
4. Click **Add Keyword**.
5. Any post on Facebook containing that word will be hidden automatically!

### Backing Up or Transferring Settings
If you use multiple computers or want to save your keyword list:
- Click **Export Settings** to save a `.json` backup file.
- On your other computer, click **Import Settings** and select the file.

---

## 🔄 How to Update the Extension

When a new version is released on GitHub:
1. Download the new ZIP file and extract it.
2. Replace the old files with the new ones in the same folder.
3. Go back to `chrome://extensions` and click the circular **Reload** icon on the extension card.
4. Refresh Facebook. Done!
